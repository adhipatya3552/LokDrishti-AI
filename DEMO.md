# LokDrishti AI Demo Outline

## Scenario

Use the preset **Monsoon market** scene. It is visually clear and triggers meaningful research dimensions without hard-coded facts:

> A night-time chase through a crowded old-city market during heavy rain, with historic architecture and narrow streets.

## Three-minute sequence

### 0:00–0:10 — Value hook
Show the scene brief and the line: “A visually perfect location can still be impossible to shoot.” Click **Scout & analyze**.

### 0:10–0:30 — Scene Intelligence
Show the structured requirements as the first agent completes: night shoot, crowded setting, historic/heritage risk, wet conditions, narrow access.

### 0:30–1:10 — Location Discovery
Show the live Parallel Search queries and candidate source links. Emphasize that candidates came from the runtime search, not a hard-coded list.

### 1:10–2:00 — Production Intelligence
Show candidate-specific queries for film permission, municipal NOC, night restrictions, heritage permission, construction, and access. Open one source link to demonstrate traceability.

### 2:00–2:35 — Evidence and ranking
Show the score breakdown: visual match, permit evidence, access, and risk. Point out any insufficient or conflicting evidence instead of calling a location “legally approved.”

### 2:35–3:00 — Decision
Show the top candidate and the disclaimer: this is evidence-backed production research; final authority confirmation remains required.
